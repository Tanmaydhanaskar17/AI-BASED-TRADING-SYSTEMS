# AI-BASED-TRADING-SYSTEMS

 This study investigates the impact of Artificial Intelligence (AI) on equity share trading, with a specific
focus on comparing market efficiency and investor behaviour before and after AI integration. Employing a
mixed-methods approach, data were collected through structured surveys from 100 respondents and secondary
sources such as financial databases. The findings indicate that AI has significantly enhanced trading efficiency
by reducing transaction costs, improving decision-making speed, and increasing returns. However, challenges
such as limited accessibility, tool complexity, and ethical concerns persist. This study also elucidates behavioural
changes among investors, including increased reliance on AI-driven platforms. Key recommendations include
simplifying AI tools, enhancing transparency, and implementing educational initiatives to support diverse
investor groups. These insights contribute to the understanding of AI's transformative role in equity trading and
provide actionable solutions to optimize its adoption
